HOW THIS WORKS

Squeektoshi runs on a real wheel in a real room. Its cadence is published, so this arcade is not a fake clock: the marker turns at the speed he is actually turning.

  1. Press START, then tap the power arc. START is the only thing that begins a shift, so you can read this page or turn the sound off first without the game running underneath you. Every clean tap on the arc is one step, and steps are the whole score.
  2. Torque is the clock. Each step winds the wheel up, and torque bleeds away every second. When it empties, the shift ends.
  3. Blocks pay. The bar under BLOCK FILL is the real block being mined right now. When a block closes while he is running, you bank a settlement bonus of one step per ten combo.
  4. Sleep stops play. When he stops, the arcade drops to a GHOST SHIFT: it replays the last live cadence and says so. Ghost steps never bank a settlement bonus.
  5. Every shift leaves a ghost. Your taps are recorded as a timeline, and the next shift races it: the small marks inside the wheel are the angles your ghost hit, and VS GHOST is the gap between you. The code on the results card carries a ghost to anyone else, no server needed.
  6. Every shift is a round. The block open when you press START is the block you played, and its state seeds the challenge: how wide the power arc is, how far it wanders and how often it swings are all derived from that block. Same block, same shift, for everyone. ROUND SEED is the first four hex of the seed, so two players on one block can compare.
  7. You can hear it. The ticks are one per quarter turn, so the tempo you hear is the tempo he is running. Sound is generated in the browser, not loaded from a file, and the button in the corner turns it off.
  8. Holders get a harder shift, not a locked one. Paste a Squeek address under HOLDER SHIFT and the power arc narrows and the swings grow, and every step you land is multiplied. It is one read of the public accounts endpoint and it is never signed, so it cannot prove the wallet is yours; it does not need to, because a tier you do not hold only makes the game harder for you. The open shift is always there, at full arc and no multiplier.

The block bar and the round seed are read from SqueekCore.state() at 0xFD6C166D4B8E2962E65746a13e5a0797CD4dE0c4 on Sepolia over a public RPC, on every poll, whether or not the API is answering, so the settled work on screen stays true. The seed is FNV-1a over sqk:<settledThroughRound>:<cumulativeWork>:<blockHeight> as trimmed hex, then mulberry32 draws the arc width, the drift and the swing count from it. One eth_call plus a few lines of arithmetic reproduces the round you played, without trusting this page. The cadence cannot be read from the chain, so when the API is silent the wheel drops to a ghost tempo and says so rather than inventing one.

Scores are kept in this browser only. Nothing is signed, nothing is spent, and no wallet is ever asked for.

HOLDER SHIFT

The arcade is free for everyone and it stays that way. If your Squeekchain profile holds something, paste it and the shift gets harder on purpose: a narrower power arc, wilder swings, and a multiplier on every step you land. It is not only Squeeks. The twenty holder collections, the builder badges and the one-of-one trophies all count, because that is what a profile actually is.

Open shift: full arc, no multiplier.

What a profile is worth, and what each tier does to the shift
1per Squeek in the wallet, per holder collection, per builder badge
2per staked Squeek, because staking is the half that does the work
3per one-of-one trophy (First Squeek, First Invitooor, First Buildooooor)
LIGHTweight 1 to 2: arc ×0.86, swings ×1.25, steps ×1.5
HEAVYweight 3 to 5: arc ×0.74, swings ×1.5, steps ×2
PRIMEweight 6 or more: arc ×0.62, swings ×1.8, steps ×2.5

An address is read through api.squeekchain.com/v1/accounts/. A member code is read through community.squeekchain.com/v1/community/profiles/, which only sends CORS headers to squeekchain.com, so api.php in this folder fetches it server-side and hands back nothing but the badge ids, the Aura and the rank. Neither read is signed, so neither can prove the profile is yours. That is on purpose: a tier you do not hold only narrows the arc and widens the swings for you, so claiming one is a handicap, not a cheat. Nothing is stored on the server, and the tier stays in this browser.
