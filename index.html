HOW THIS WORKS

Squeektoshi runs on a real wheel in a real room. Its cadence is published, so this arcade is not a fake clock: the marker turns at the speed he is actually turning.

  1. Tap the power arc. Every clean tap is one step. Steps are the whole score.
  2. Torque is the clock. Each step winds the wheel up, and torque bleeds away every second. When it empties, the shift ends.
  3. Blocks pay. The bar under BLOCK FILL is the real block being mined right now. When a block closes while he is running, you bank a settlement bonus of one step per ten combo.
  4. Sleep stops play. When he stops, the arcade drops to a GHOST SHIFT: it replays the last live cadence and says so. Ghost steps never bank a settlement bonus.
